Fast. Private. Secure.

Personal Lift does away with complex accounts, forced subscriptions, and clunky interfaces. Built from the ground up to be fast, private, and secure, the app runs entirely on your device. Whether you are aiming for a new squat milestone, calculating plate math, or monitoring Wilks and DOTS scores, Personal Lift adapts automatically to optimize your experience.

Core Features

Plate Math

Barbell Plate Calculator

No more gym math. Enter your target intensity and the app instantly calculates the exact plate arrangement for each side of the bar.

The Big Three

Core Lifts & Maxes

Track 1RM benchmarks for Squat, Bench Press, and Deadlift. Watch your strength adapt dynamically as you log new achievements.

Relative Strength

Wilks & DOTS Scoring

Instantly compute powerlifting coefficients based on body weight, gender, and total lifts to benchmark your relative strength index.

Structure

Preset & Custom Routines

Launch quickly with built-in templates like the "Powerlifting Big Three" or build custom volume routines for your training week.

Recovery

Integrated Rest Timer

Stay focused with a floating capsule-style rest timer that overlays on screen, keeping rest periods precise.

Health

Apple Health Integration

Seamlessly sync logged lifting sessions and calories burned to monitor your daily activity and close your rings.

OLED-Optimized Night Mode

Choose from light, dark, or pitch-black OLED layouts. An elegant theme selector helps you calibrate your preference before you lift.

Tactile Haptics

Feel the weight of your workouts with custom-tailored Taptic engine vibrations on key actions and logs.
